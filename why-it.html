Why IT?

Studying HBO-ICT is a risk for me, because it takes me outside my usual comfort zone. Before starting this programme, I had only worked with code a handful of times. Each of those times was something I chose to do out of my own interest, and I enjoyed it. That enjoyment was a major reason for pursuing a bachelor in ICT. As long as I keep showing up and completing my tasks, I believe I will come to understand the material and continue to find it interesting.

Why HZ?

I chose HZ University of Applied Sciences because of the small-scale education, the location and the practical way of education.

My long-term plan

In the next 4 years I want to finish my bachelor and build a portfolio of projects, including small games. During my studies I want to gain team experience through the projects. After graduating, I want to work as a junior game developer, and in 4-5 years I want to grow into a developer who contributes to both the design and technical side of a game
